Azrael

⸻

Overview

Azrael is the central figure of the Omniversal Project.

He is an Omniversal Adventurer whose existence spans countless realities, worlds, and timelines. While each universe may interpret him differently, the individual known as Azrael remains connected by a consistent core identity.

Azrael adapts to the universe he inhabits without losing the experiences, values, and characteristics that define him.

⸻

Basic Information

Name: Azrael

Role: Omniversal Adventurer

Classification: Omniversal Constant

Species: Universe-dependent

Primary Archetypes: Sorcerer, Necromancer, Blood Mage, Death Mage, Lich, Spellblade/Spellsword

⸻

Character Concept

Azrael represents the idea of a single individual adapting across infinite realities while remaining fundamentally the same person.

Each universe presents a different chapter of his existence, allowing him to interact with different cultures, histories, conflicts, technologies, magical systems, and philosophies while preserving the core elements that define him.

His identity is not dependent upon any single world’s interpretation of him.

⸻

Core Identity

Azrael is an arcane scholar, warrior, and protector who has spent his existence pursuing knowledge and surviving conflicts across the Omniverse.

He possesses extensive knowledge of magic and is willing to employ dangerous, forbidden, or otherwise taboo forms of power when necessary.

His use of such powers does not inherently define him as evil. Azrael believes that power is ultimately defined by how it is used and the responsibility carried by the individual wielding it.

He is capable of wielding forces associated with death, blood, necromancy, and other forms of forbidden magic while maintaining a strong sense of restraint and responsibility.

⸻

Omniversal Identity

Azrael is not defined by a single universe, species, title, ability, or incarnation.

His identity is defined by his experiences, choices, values, and the consistent traits that remain across realities.

His species, appearance, abilities, equipment, and circumstances may change according to the universe he inhabits, but these adaptations do not make him a different individual.

The Omniverse changes around him, but the person at its center remains Azrael.

⸻

Role Within the Omniverse

Azrael serves as both an explorer and a connection point between different worlds and stories.

His journey allows him to experience and interact with countless universes while respecting their individual lore, cultures, histories, themes, and established rules.

He does not exist to replace the heroes or legends of the worlds he visits. Instead, his presence allows their stories to intersect with his own while preserving their individual identities.

⸻

Abilities and Knowledge

Azrael possesses extensive knowledge and experience in both arcane and martial disciplines.

His established areas of expertise include:

* Sorcery.
* Necromancy.
* Death magic.
* Blood magic.
* Forbidden and taboo magic.
* Alchemy.
* Chronomancy.
* Spellblade and spellsword combat.
* Arcane scholarship.

His abilities and the way they manifest may vary according to the rules of the universe he inhabits.

Azrael also possesses unique abilities that transcend individual universe-specific systems, including Omnivision. The detailed nature and limitations of this ability remain subject to further canon development.

⸻

Core Philosophy

Azrael believes that knowledge and power carry responsibility.

He does not reject forbidden knowledge simply because it is feared or condemned by others. Instead, he evaluates its nature, consequences, and purpose.

This philosophy allows him to walk the boundary between forces traditionally considered opposing:

* Life and death.
* Magic and warfare.
* Knowledge and danger.
* Darkness and protection.
* Power and restraint.

Despite the nature of many of the powers he commands, Azrael’s purpose is ultimately protective.

⸻

Project Role

Within the Omniversal Project, Azrael serves as the central character through whom countless worlds, stories, and legends can intersect.

His journey provides a framework for exploring different universes without erasing their individuality.

The Omniversal Project uses Azrael’s journey to connect stories while maintaining respect for the worlds, characters, histories, and themes that make each reality distinct.
