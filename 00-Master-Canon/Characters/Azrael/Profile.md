Azrael

⸻

Overview

Azrael is the central figure of the Omniversal Project.

He is an Omniversal Adventurer whose existence spans countless realities, worlds, and timelines. While each universe may interpret him differently, the individual known as Azrael remains connected by a consistent core identity.

⸻

Basic Information

Name: Azrael

Role: Omniversal Adventurer

Classification: Omniversal Constant

Species: Universe-dependent

⸻

Character Concept

Azrael represents the idea of a single individual adapting across infinite realities while remaining fundamentally the same person.

Each universe presents a different chapter of his existence, allowing him to interact with different cultures, histories, conflicts, and philosophies while preserving the core elements that define him.

⸻

Omniversal Identity

Azrael is not defined by a single universe, species, title, or ability.

His identity is defined by his experiences, choices, and the consistent traits that remain across realities.

The Omniverse changes around him, but the person at its center remains Azrael.

⸻

Project Role

Within the Omniversal Project, Azrael serves as a connection point between different worlds and stories.

His journey allows the exploration of various universes while maintaining respect for their individual lore, themes, and established rules.
