Azrael — Visual Reference Sheet

Status: Candidate Master Canon — Visual Reference
Purpose: Image generation, image-to-video, video generation, character turnarounds, and visual continuity
Scope: Visual appearance and externally relevant equipment only.

This document is the concise visual reference for Azrael. Detailed mechanical, magical, technological, and defensive systems belong in expanded canon documentation.

⸻

1. Core Character

Name: Azrael
Species: Adult male anthropomorphic dragon
Default Height: 9’6”
Build: Athletic and powerful; broad-shouldered and muscular without a bodybuilder physique.

Overall Silhouette

Azrael’s baseline silhouette consists of:

* Tall humanoid-dragon proportions.
* Broad shoulders, chest, and arms.
* Thick neck.
* Large hands.
* Large biological wings.
* Long, tapered biological tail.
* Close-fitting dark armor.
* Dark hooded mantle when worn.

His silhouette should remain imposing and recognizable without becoming excessively bulky.

⸻

2. Body & Coloration

Scales

* Matte black.

Underbelly

* Rich purple.
* This is a natural biological coloration, not an armor color.

Eyes

* Left eye: Emerald green.
* Right eye: Crimson red.

The heterochromia is a major identifying feature.

Horns

* Prominent natural horns.
* Fully concealed when the mantle hood is worn.
* Horns should not visibly protrude through the hood.

Face

* Natural dragon facial anatomy.
* Mature masculine facial structure.
* Teeth are not visible when the mouth is closed.

Scars

* Subtle old battle scars.
* They should remain understated rather than dominating the character’s appearance.

Tattoos

* None established in the current baseline.

⸻

3. Wings

Azrael’s normal wings are large biological wings.

* Natural anatomical structures.
* Not mechanical by default.
* Integrated directly into his body.
* Capable of natural folding, movement, and flight.
* Armor accommodates the wings without requiring an external support system.
* No standard wing harness, rig, or equipment frame.

Wing Armor

* Folded wings can receive integrated protection.
* Wing membranes receive selective protection while retaining flexibility.
* Wing edges receive flexible protection.
* Armor follows the natural wing structure rather than turning the wings into mechanical components.

⸻

4. Tail

Azrael has a long, tapered biological tail.

* Naturally flexible and nimble.
* Used for balance and movement.
* Maintains natural biological appearance.
* Tail armor integrates with its anatomy.
* Tail tip is reinforced.
* No standard equipment is attached to the tail.

The tail should not be visually transformed into a mechanical or heavily accessorized appendage unless a specific alternate-universe version establishes that change.

⸻

5. Omniversal Armor

Azrael’s normal armor is the Omniversal Armor.

Overall Construction

* Close-fitting.
* Form-fitting.
* Physical armor.
* Hybrid construction of physical armor plates and a cohesive armored underlayer.
* Moderate segmentation.
* Balanced protection, mobility, and durability.
* Lightweight-to-moderate visual weight.
* Anatomically shaped to Azrael rather than based on generic humanoid armor.

Major Areas

* Single continuous primary chest plate.
* Integrated abdomen.
* Integrated lower torso.
* Hybrid back and wing-root protection.
* Integrated pelvic and waist transitions.
* Hybrid shoulder and upper-arm protection.
* Integrated forearm/gauntlet protection.
* Minimal hand armor.
* Functional exposed claws.
* Integrated leg armor.
* Armored footwear.
* Integrated tail-root protection.
* Flexible wing protection.

Surface

* Predominantly matte.
* Selective satin/semi-gloss surfaces.
* Subtle tonal variation within the black.
* Predominantly black overall.
* Subtle green and red detailing.
* No deliberate purple armor coloration.

Magic & Technology

Magic and technology are integrated into the armor without visibly separating into two different systems.

* Technological components are concealed.
* No exposed circuitry.
* No visible electronic panels.
* No obvious LEDs.
* No conventional futuristic interface elements.
* Magical runes/effects are minimal and generally concealed.

The armor should visually read as a unified dark-fantasy system, not conventional sci-fi armor.

⸻

6. Mantle

Azrael’s outer garment is a mantle, not a robe.

General Design

* Dark charcoal/black.
* Slightly loose.
* Mobile and practical.
* Mid-calf length.
* Designed to avoid snagging during movement.
* Allows the armor to remain visible.

Hood

* Normally worn up when the mantle is part of the configuration.
* Completely conceals the horns.
* Creates a strong hooded silhouette.
* Does not require visible mechanical components.

Sleeves

* Extend from the shoulder to the elbow.
* Do not function as full-length robe sleeves.

Front Opening

Adaptive variable opening.

* Normally partially open.
* Keeps the armor visibly identifiable.
* Can naturally adjust with movement.

Tail Integration

Fully adaptive tail integration.

* Accommodates the tail’s position and movement.
* Material can separate, wrap, or shift naturally.
* No obvious permanent tail opening when the tail is stationary.
* Avoids snagging during combat, climbing, running, or flight.
* Preserves the mantle’s mid-calf silhouette.

General Visual Rule

The mantle must continue to read as a functional hooded mantle, not a robe, cape, armor system, or force field.

⸻

7. Mask

The mask is a situational alternate equipment state rather than Azrael’s default facial appearance.

Design Foundation

The mask follows a custom construction strongly inspired by Darth Revan’s mask, with Mandalorian influence inherited through that design lineage.

It is:

* An individualized design.
* Dark-fantasy/military in construction.
* Adapted to Azrael’s anatomy.
* Not a direct copy of Revan’s mask.

Construction

* Predominantly black.
* Predominantly matte.
* Compact.
* Rigid physical construction.
* Strong angular geometry.
* Layered Revan-inspired construction.
* Strong angular beveling.
* Revan-weighted jaw and muzzle geometry.
* Dragon anatomy accommodated internally rather than emphasized as a conventional dragon muzzle.
* Symmetrical overall design.
* Subtle functional asymmetry only where anatomy/function requires it.
* Continuous central ridge.
* Layered cheek, chin, eye, temple, jaw, and lower-face structures.
* Revan-inspired structural seams and hardware.
* Integrated ventilation elements.

Eyes

* Natural eyes remain visible and recognizable.
* No visor.
* No artificial lenses.
* No HUD.

Hood Integration

* Mask and hood function as a unified concealed-head configuration.
* Horns are completely concealed.
* Hood remains dominant over the upper/rear head.
* Mask concentrates around the face.

Voice

* No mechanical voice modulator.
* The mask naturally alters/muffles Azrael’s voice.

Use & Storage

* Physically donned and removed.
* Subtle assistance may aid alignment/fastening.
* No visible locking or activation indicators.
* Removed mask is stored completely within the mantle.
* Storage does not create an obvious bulge or silhouette change.

The utility belt is part of Azrael’s standard visible equipment.

Design

* Hybrid dark-fantasy construction.
* Military practicality.
* Adventurer functionality.
* Compatible with the Omniversal Armor.
* Restrained rather than overloaded.

Configuration

Buckle

11. Spellblade

Spellblade is a combat discipline, not a physical weapon.

Being described as a spellblade does not mean Azrael should automatically be depicted carrying a sword.

Any physical weapon is situational and must be explicitly established by the relevant scene, universe, or loadout.

12. Situational Equipment

Azrael can acquire or use additional equipment based on:

* Universe.
* Mission.
* Environment.
* Circumstances.
* Local technology.
* Local culture.
* Local military or adventuring conventions.

Situational equipment can substantially replace his normal equipment.

Alternate-Universe Rule

When equipment is replaced or heavily adapted for another universe:

* The equipment may fully adopt that universe’s visual language.
* It does not require an explicit Azrael-specific visual marker.
* His normal equipment aesthetics do not need to be forcibly carried into every universe.
* The equipment must still accommodate his established physical anatomy.

⸻

13. Equipment Continuity

For ordinary scenes, equipment follows a continuity-first rule.

* Equipment remains consistent between scenes unless a change is explicitly established.
* Image/video sequences should not randomly add or remove equipment.
* Major equipment changes should be intentional and contextually established.
* Persistent equipment should maintain its established placement.

15. Visual Style Direction

Azrael’s preferred visual direction is:

Dark-fantasy graphic-novel/comic-book illustration with painterly rendering and semi-realistic anatomy.

Key characteristics:

* Bold ink contours.
* Painterly comic rendering.
* Stylized semi-realistic anatomy.
* Controlled cel-shading.
* Textured brushwork.
* Strong silhouettes.
* Deep blacks.
* Purple accents primarily through Azrael’s natural underbelly.
* Expressive eyes and facial expressions.
* Cinematic composition.
* Dramatic but believable lighting.
* Detailed materials without excessive visual noise.
* Grounded anatomy appropriate to the scene.

The style should translate effectively to both still imagery and motion/video.
