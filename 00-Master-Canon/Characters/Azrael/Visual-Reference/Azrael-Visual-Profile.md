1. Core Identity

Name: Azrael

Species: Adult male anthropomorphic dragon

Default Height: 9’6”

Build: Athletic and powerful; broad shoulders, broad chest, strong arms, thick neck, large hands. Powerful without excessive bodybuilding proportions.

Overall Silhouette: Large, imposing humanoid-dragon silhouette with broad upper body, large biological wings, long tapered tail, fitted armor, and hooded mantle.

Baseline Presentation: Intimidating or stern at rest without requiring an overtly aggressive expression.

⸻

2. Anatomy

Scales

* Matte black.
* Predominantly consistent across the body.
* No unnecessary decorative patterning.

Underbelly

* Rich purple.
* Natural biological coloration.
* Primary source of purple in the baseline character design.

Eyes

* Left eye: emerald green.
* Right eye: crimson red.
* Natural eyes.
* No baseline artificial lenses, HUDs, or visor effects.

Face

* Natural dragon facial anatomy.
* Teeth are not visible when the mouth is closed.
* No unnecessary exaggerated snarl or aggressive facial distortion.

Horns

* Prominent natural horns.
* Visible when the hood is not covering them.
* Fully concealed by the mantle hood when the hood is up.

Wings

* Large biological wings.
* Natural anatomical construction.
* Not mechanical by default.
* Integrated directly with the body and armor.
* No external support harness or mechanical wing framework.

Tail

* Long, tapered, biological tail.
* Nimble and functional.
* Used for balance and movement.
* Integrated tail armor.
* Reinforced tail tip.
* No standard external equipment attached to the tail.

Scars

* Subtle old battle scars.
* Integrated naturally into the anatomy.
* Not exaggerated or visually dominant.

Tattoos

* None established in the current baseline design.

3. Armor Profile

Role: Baseline protective combat armor.

Construction:

* Close-fitting.
* Form-fitting.
* Physical armor.
* Hybrid plate and cohesive armored-underlayer construction.
* Moderate segmentation.
* Anatomically shaped.

Visual Weight:

* Lightweight-to-moderate.
* Protective without appearing excessively bulky.
* Designed to preserve mobility and the underlying anatomy.

Color:

* Predominantly black.
* Subtle tonal variation.
* Predominantly matte.
* Selective satin/semi-gloss surfaces where appropriate.
* No deliberate purple armor coloration.
* Subtle green and red detailing may be present.

Technology:

* Technological elements are completely concealed.
* No visible circuitry.
* No exposed panels.
* No LEDs.
* No obvious futuristic interfaces.
* Magic and technology are visually integrated rather than separately exposed.

Magic:

* Runes and magical elements are minimal and generally concealed.
* Avoid excessive glowing magical ornamentation.

Anatomical Integration:

* Continuous chest and abdomen protection.
* Integrated lower torso.
* Hybrid back and wing-root protection.
* Integrated pelvic, waist, and tail-root transitions.
* Hybrid shoulder and upper-arm protection.
* Integrated forearm and gauntlet protection.
* Minimal hand armor.
* Claws remain functional.
* Integrated leg and armored-footwear protection.
* Flexible wing and tail articulation.

4. Mantle Profile

Garment Type: Mantle.

Important Terminology: It is a mantle, not a robe.

Color: Dark charcoal/black.

Fit: Slightly loose while remaining mobile and practical.

Length: Mid-calf.

Sleeves: Shoulder-to-elbow length.

Hood:

* Fully conceals the horns when raised.
* Creates a strong hooded silhouette.
* Integrates naturally with the mask when the mask is equipped.

Front Opening:

* Adaptive variable opening.
* Normally partially open so the armor remains visible.
* Construction naturally adjusts with movement.

Tail Integration:

* Fully adaptive.
* Accommodates tail position and movement.
* Material can separate, wrap, or shift around the tail.
* No obvious permanent tail opening when the tail is stationary.
* Must not snag during combat, climbing, running, or flight.
* Maintains the intended mid-calf silhouette.

Visual Function:

* Outer silhouette layer.
* Concealment and environmental protection.
* Provides visual contrast against the fitted armor.
* Must not visually become armor, a cape, or a robe.

⸻

5. Mask Profile

Availability: Situational alternate equipment state.

Primary Inspiration: Darth Revan’s mask, with Mandalorian influence through Revan’s construction.

Design Direction: Individualized dark-fantasy/military interpretation rather than a direct reproduction.

Construction

* Rigid physical mask.
* Compact.
* Angular.
* Layered construction.
* Predominantly Revan-inspired geometry.
* Adapted to Azrael’s dragon anatomy.
* Revan-weighted jaw and muzzle structure.
* Revan-inspired central ridge.
* Layered cheek, chin, eye, and facial structures.
* Strong angular beveling.
* Fundamentally symmetrical.
* Subtle asymmetry only where anatomy/function requires it.

Face Coverage

* Enclosed lower face.
* No visible mouth opening.
* Dragon mouth concealed internally.
* Natural dragon anatomy accommodated beneath the mask.

Eyes

* Natural eyes remain recognizable.
* Eye openings use angular Revan-inspired geometry adapted to Azrael’s proportions.
* No HUD.
* No artificial visor.
* No glowing artificial lenses.

Hood Integration

* Mask and hood form a unified concealed-head configuration.
* Horns remain concealed.
* Forehead and upper-head transition is hood-dominant.
* Rear structure is primarily concealed by the hood.

Ventilation

* Revan/Mandalorian-inspired structural vents.
* Integrated into the facial geometry.
* Functional and visually understated.
* Not a modern respirator or external life-support apparatus.

Voice

* No mechanical voice modulator.
* Natural voice with physical muffling caused by the mask.

Technology

* Functional technology may be integrated internally.
* No visible circuitry, LEDs, or external futuristic interfaces.

Storage

* Stored within the mantle when removed.
* Storage is completely concealed.
* No visible bulge or silhouette change.

Donning/Removal

* Manually worn and removed.
* Internal assistance may subtly align, secure, or release the mask.
* No visible activation or lock indicators.

9. Spellblade Representation

Critical Rule:

Spellblade is a combat/magical discipline, not a physical weapon.

Do not automatically give Azrael a sword, blade, or other dedicated weapon simply because he is described as a spellblade.

Physical weapons are separate equipment decisions.

Visible spellblade manifestations may appear when specifically relevant to the scene.

Dark-fantasy graphic-novel/comic-book illustration combined with polished semi-realistic digital painting.

Rendering

* Painterly rendering.
* Semi-realistic anatomy.
* Controlled cel-shading.
* Textured brushwork.
* Bold ink contours.
* Strong silhouettes.
* Detailed material rendering.
* Controlled visual noise.

Color Direction

* Deep blacks.
* Natural rich purple from the underbelly.
* Subtle green and red accents.
* Strong contrast.
* Avoid unnecessary saturated colors.

Lighting

* Cinematic.
* Dramatic but believable.

Composition

* Cinematic framing.
