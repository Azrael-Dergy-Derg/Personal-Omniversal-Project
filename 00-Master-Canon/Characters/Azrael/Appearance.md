Azrael — Appearance

⸻

General Description

Azrael maintains a recognizable appearance across the Omniverse. While his species and anatomy may change to fit the universe he inhabits, several visual characteristics remain consistent and serve as his defining features.

His primary Omniversal appearance is that of a large, imposing anthropomorphic dragon with a battle-worn but disciplined presence.

⸻

Physical Characteristics

Primary Features

* Large, imposing stature.
* Broad shoulders and an athletic, powerful physique.
* Predominantly dark coloration.
* Distinctive mismatched eyes.
* Prominent horns.
* Normal biological wings.
* A biological, tapered, and nimble tail.
* Battle-worn appearance reflecting years of conflict and survival.
* Visible scars earned throughout his lifetime.

Height

Azrael’s height varies depending on the universe and species interpretation.

His primary Omniversal dragon form is approximately 1.5 times the height of his human inspiration, with larger depictions reaching approximately 9’6”.

Universe-specific incarnations may differ according to the setting and its established species or anatomical conventions.

⸻

Coloration

Azrael’s most recognizable coloration includes:

* Predominantly matte-black scales.
* A rich purple underbelly and secondary coloration.
* Contrasting eye colors:
    * One emerald-green eye.
    * One crimson-red eye.

These features serve as consistent visual identifiers across different interpretations of the character.

⸻

Eyes

Azrael possesses heterochromia.

* Left eye: Emerald green
* Right eye: Crimson red

His mismatched eyes are among his most recognizable physical features and remain consistent across nearly all interpretations of the character.

⸻

Head and Facial Features

Azrael possesses prominent dragon horns and a distinct dragon-like snout.

His primary design uses the established Snout B configuration.

When wearing his hood, Azrael’s horns are concealed beneath it. His hooded silhouette is intentionally ambiguous: unless his face or tail is visible, it should not be immediately apparent that he is a dragon rather than another humanoid being.

⸻

Scars and Markings

Azrael carries physical reminders of his experiences.

These include:

* Battle scars from countless conflicts.
* Universe-specific markings when appropriate to the setting.
* Other physical signs of his long history and experiences.

Azrael has no tattoos in his current canonical physical design.

This remains subject to future revision if specifically established as canon.

⸻

Clothing and Armor Style

Azrael’s overall appearance favors a combination of:

* Heavy protective armor.
* Practical combat clothing.
* Layered designs.
* Ancient and modern influences combined together.
* Functional garments designed for mobility and combat.

His visual style reflects the combination of:

* Warrior.
* Scholar.
* Adventurer.
* Battlemage.

His clothing is functional first, designed for survival and combat rather than decoration.

His primary Omniversal equipment includes his Omniversal Armor and mantle. The mantle serves as a distinctive outer garment and contributes significantly to his recognizable silhouette.

⸻

Visual Theme

Azrael’s appearance embodies the balance between opposing forces:

* Darkness and restraint.
* Ancient knowledge and modern practicality.
* Magic and warfare.
* Intimidation and protection.

His design communicates that he is someone who has endured countless battles while choosing responsibility over destruction.

His appearance is intended to evoke both danger and protection: an imposing figure capable of wielding considerable power, yet defined by discipline and restraint rather than uncontrolled violence.

⸻

Canon Notes

* Azrael’s primary Omniversal form is an anthropomorphic dragon.
* His species and anatomy may change when adapting to individual universes.
* His core visual identifiers should remain recognizable across those adaptations whenever the setting permits.
* His current canonical physical design contains no tattoos.
* His primary design uses normal biological wings and a biological tapered tail.
* His horns are prominent but are concealed when his hood is properly worn.
* The hooded design intentionally obscures his species when his face and tail are not visible.
